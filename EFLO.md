# EFLO — tools/list measurement

Generated by `scripts/eflo_measure.py` (`just eflo`). Do not edit by
hand; re-run the script.

- Server: meister 0.2.0, the built binary over stdio, one
  modern-era session per profile (`MEISTER_PROFILE`).
- Method: the served `tools` array, taken from the wire and
  canonicalised as compact JSON with sorted keys, raw UTF-8.
- Tokenizer: `tiktoken` 0.14.0, `cl100k_base`. Real counts; no
  estimate appears in this file.
- Budget (fleet EFLO, decision D5): **≤ 2,500 tokens** per served
  surface. The Budget column states the arithmetic only; a waiver
  or a split is the Owner's ruling, recorded in COMPLIANCE.md §3.
